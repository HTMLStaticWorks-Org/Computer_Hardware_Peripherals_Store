Field Engineering Protocol

White-Glove Custom Rig Assembly & Validation Lifecycle

From initial software workload profiling to physical clearance dry-fitting, firmware curve optimization, and 48-hour burn-in stress audits.

Lifecycle Step 01

Workload Profiling & Architecture Match

We match your exact software pipeline (Unreal Engine 5, DaVinci Resolve Studio, Blender 3D, or 240Hz competitive esports) to the optimal CPU core hierarchy, VRAM headroom, and PCIe 5.0 lane distribution.

Workload Sizing • Bottleneck Analysis
Lifecycle Step 02

Precision Bench Dry-Fit & Clearance Audit

Components undergo hands-on assembly dry-fitting on our Austin studio test bench. We confirm millimeter GPU clearance, front/top radiator tube bend radius, and zero conflict with tall DDR5 heatsinks.

Hands-On Test Bench • 0mm Clearance Errors

BIOS Microcode, Curve Optimizer & Timings

Motherboard UEFI is updated to the latest validated microcode. Technicians apply per-core curve optimizer negative offsets, tune custom zero-RPM PWM fan curves, and tighten memory sub-timings.
